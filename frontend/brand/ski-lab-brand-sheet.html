Ski Lab

The mountain, measured. A trip-optimization engine, not a travel blog — the identity should read like an instrument, not a brochure: precise marks, real data, no decoration that isn't also information.

Primary lockup

SKI LAB

The mark is a measurement, not a mountain-for-its-own-sake: a peak silhouette crossed by a surveyor's line with tick marks, and an amber sample point at the summit — literally "the mountain, sampled." The LAB tag reuses the same pill/chip shape already used for status indicators throughout the app (see "Guest" chip below), so the logo and the UI share one visual vocabulary instead of two.

Usage

Do
  • Use the amber point only once per view — it marks the single most important number.
  • Keep the icon's stroke gradient blue→amber; it echoes the elevation line motif used elsewhere.
  • Set "LAB" in the pill tag at minimum 9px mono, always uppercase.
Don't
  • Don't recolor Piste Red/Blue for anything except terrain data — they'd stop meaning what they mean.
  • Don't add a drop shadow or bevel to the icon — flat, instrument-panel, not skeuomorphic.
  • Don't set the wordmark in any face but Space Grotesk, even at small sizes.
