# Ski Lab — Ad Kit (2026-08-29)

Three finished static ads live in `ads/` (PNG, retina 2x, plus the
HTML sources they were rendered from). Every number in them is a real,
measured figure from the product — never invented:

- **€992 Gudauri** — live total found by an actual search on the
  production app, 2026-08-29 (7 nights, per person, everything in).
- **€1,620 vs €1,722 Val Thorens** — the engine's measured shoulder
  (Dec 1–18) vs peak (Dec 20+) totals; €102 apart.
- **39 resorts** — the current database.

### Video 1 — "The Line" (hero, 10s)
> Aerial drone shot at dawn over untouched alpine powder, deep blue
> hour light. A single skier carves one clean, confident line down the
> face. The camera rises; the carved line begins to GLOW soft electric
> blue (#2563eb) and extends ahead of the skier, becoming a plotted
> route with waypoint dots down the whole mountain. Cool navy color
> grade, cinematic, crisp spindrift backlit. No text.
- End card (add in edit): ink background, "החופשה כולה. מחיר אחד. אמיתי." /
  "Ski Lab — ski-lab-app.web.app". Logo type: Montserrat 800.

### Video 2 — "40 tabs" (social cut, 8s)
> Screen-recording style: a chaotic browser with dozens of overlapping
> tabs (flights, hotels, transfer quotes, a spreadsheet). The tabs
> tremble, then get sucked away in a whoosh toward one clean white
> card in the center of a dark navy screen. The card flips over to
> reveal a mountain vista through it, like a window. Fast, satisfying,
> slightly playful motion design. UI aesthetic: white cards, blue
> accents, rounded corners.
- End card: "40 טאבים. או חיפוש אחד." + URL.

### Video 3 — "Different Saturday" (performance cut, 8s)
> A single majestic snow-covered mountain, fixed camera, timelapse:
> clouds stream past, light shifts across weeks, a subtle calendar of
> Saturdays flickering in the corner. On the final beat the light goes
> golden and settles. Cool navy grade throughout, minimal, elegant.
- Overlay in edit: "€1,722 → €1,620 · same slopes" then
  "Ski Lab finds the week that's actually cheaper."

## Claims discipline

Do not add claims the product can't back: no "cheapest guaranteed",
no invented savings percentages, no fake reviews. The honest angles
(real total price, real per-line provenance, flexible-date engine)
are the differentiators — every quoted figure above is traceable to a
measured search.
